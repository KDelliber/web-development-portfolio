Technician Playbook

Delliber Service Desk · Structured training methodology
Service Desk Portfolio Project
Used during a simulated IT help desk training lab—not professional employment experience. This playbook keeps troubleshooting, verification, and documentation consistent across practice tickets.
Call Flow

Guide the support conversation

Listen→Clarify→Isolate→Fix→Verify→Document

Ticket Notes

Cause → Action → Result

CauseWhat was wrong?
ActionWhat did I do?
ResultDid it work, and how did I verify it?
Troubleshooting Flow

Work from evidence

Scope→Reproduce→Simplest Cause→Isolate→Change One Thing→Retest

Before Escalating

Send a complete handoff

  • What failed?
  • What still works?
  • What have I already tested?
  • What is the exact error or message?
  • What is the business impact or deadline?
  • Which team owns the issue?
Verification Reminder

Verify the user’s actual task

Do not stop at “it works.” Verify the actual user task.
  • Open another file
  • Print another document
  • Send and receive a test email
  • Reconnect to the drive
  • Confirm the application works normally
